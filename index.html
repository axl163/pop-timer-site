Pop Timer for Catchers

Measure a throw-down from video, then export the clip with the pop and exchange labels on it.

What it does

  • Import a throw from Photos. The video stays on the iPhone.
  • Mark mitt catch, release, and target catch. A sound chart helps you find those moments. It does not set the marks.
  • Exchange is mitt catch to release. Pop is mitt catch to target catch.
  • History keeps the date, the exchange, and the pop for each throw.
  • Export writes those times onto the full clip. Save the video from the share sheet when you want it in Photos.

Privacy

Pop Timer reads videos you choose from Photos so you can mark exchange and pop times. Those videos stay on your iPhone. Pop Timer does not create an account, and it does not send those videos to a server.
